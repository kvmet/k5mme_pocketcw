
==============================================================================
F000400.bin, the PocketCW content pack
==============================================================================

The content pack includes data and speech from these works.

Word list: wordfreq 3.1.1 by Robyn Speer, https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq
Its data is under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
PocketCW kept the 3000 most frequent English words made only of letters,
removed single letters other than A and I and the words in
tools/words/blocklist.txt, and upper cased them. That list, in the pack and as
tools/words/common.txt in the PocketCW source, is shared under the same
license. The pack leaves out the least common words that do not fit.

wordfreq's data comes from many sources. Its credits include:
- SUBTLEX, by Marc Brysbaert, Boris New and the other SUBTLEX authors.
  SUBTLEX is freely available data.
- OpenSubtitles, https://www.opensubtitles.org
- Google Books Ngram Viewer, http://books.google.com/ngrams
The full list is in wordfreq's README.

Voice: Kokoro-82M by hexgrad, voice af_heart,
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
The model is under the Apache License 2.0. The clips in the pack are speech
that the model made.
